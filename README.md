# 🌍 GlobalMedHealth Excel Data Analytics Project

A comprehensive **Microsoft Excel Data Analytics & Business Analysis portfolio project** focused on helping **GlobalMedHealth Systems** identify the most suitable countries for international healthcare expansion through data-driven decision making.

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used beyond spreadsheets to perform **business analysis, data cleaning, scoring models, strategic recommendations, and executive reporting.**

---

# 📖 Project Overview

GlobalMedHealth Systems plans to expand its healthcare operations internationally.

The objective of this project was to analyze multiple business datasets to identify the countries offering the highest strategic potential based on:

- Healthcare Demand
- Economic Viability
- Population
- Healthcare Infrastructure
- GDP
- Growth Potential

After evaluating all available data, three countries were recommended for expansion based on comprehensive business analysis.

- 🇦🇺 Australia
- 🇩🇪 Germany
- 🇮🇳 India

---
